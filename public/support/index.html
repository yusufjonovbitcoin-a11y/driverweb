What do you need help with?

Choose a topic for a few useful steps.

Sign-in & account accessGet back into your account
  1. Check that you’re using the email address associated with your fleet account and that your device is online.
  2. Use Forgot password? on the sign-in screen if you need to reset your password. Check your inbox and spam folder.
  3. Ask your fleet administrator about invitations, company access, or an inactive account. Contact app support if sign-in still fails, with the error text but never your password or verification code.
Loads & trip progressAssignments, stops, and status
  1. Open your loads while connected to the internet and check that you’re signed into the correct fleet account.
  2. If an assignment or a pickup/delivery detail is missing, confirm it with your dispatcher. They manage your assigned work.
  3. Follow the current stop’s actions in order. If a status action is unavailable, check its message and any required BOL or POD before trying again.
Documents & uploadsBOL, POD, files, and AI checks
  1. Select the correct load, stop, and document type. Use a readable PDF or clear photo, and follow the file limits shown in the app.
  2. Wait for the upload to finish and confirm that the document appears. A queued or checking AI status is separate from the file’s upload status; review any warnings against the original.
  3. If a saved file won’t open, reconnect and reopen it to request a fresh link. If an upload failed, keep the original and read the error before retrying. Don’t email confidential load files to support.
Maps & locationDestinations and device permissions
  1. Check your connection and open the map for the intended load. Confirm pickup and delivery addresses with your dispatcher if they look wrong.
  2. Turn on device location services and review T Fleets location permissions in your device settings.
  3. Background tracking depends on device permissions, battery settings, and connectivity. Check the time of the last location update; don’t assume an older point is live.
NotificationsWhen an alert doesn’t arrive
  1. Allow notifications for T Fleets in your device settings and check Focus, Do Not Disturb, and battery restrictions.
  2. Open the app while online and check its notifications and messages directly. Device alerts can be delayed or suppressed.
  3. If the issue continues, tell support your device model, OS version, and the approximate time and time zone of the missing alert. Use your usual dispatcher contact for urgent trip changes.
Messages & connectionSending, syncing, and calling
  1. Check your connection and the message’s send status before sending the same message again. Use the retry action if the app shows one.
  2. For calls or voice messages, check microphone permissions. Video calls also need camera access.
  3. Keep the app and your original files until pending work is confirmed as sent. Clearing app data or reinstalling can remove local drafts; contact support before using either as a troubleshooting step.

A useful report starts with a few details.

No long form. A short description helps us understand the issue and where it happened.

  • App version, if known, plus device model and OS version.
  • The screen you were on and what you expected to happen.
  • The exact error, approximate time and time zone, and steps to repeat it.
  • A cropped, redacted screenshot if it helps explain the issue.

Keep passwords, verification codes, access tokens, payment details, and confidential Rate Con, BOL, or POD files out of support emails. For questions about personal information, see the Privacy Policy. For immediate trip assistance, contact your dispatcher; app support is not an emergency service.
