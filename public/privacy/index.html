Privacy, in context

T Fleets connects drivers with their fleet operations. This policy is intended to explain how information relates to accounts, assigned loads, trip location, and communications, and where to direct privacy requests.

01

About this policy

This policy covers the T Fleets mobile application and the information processed in connection with its driver and fleet features. References to “we,” “us,” and “our” will refer to the verified operator of T Fleets.

If you use T Fleets through a fleet or employer, that organization may also determine how your work-related information is used. Ask your fleet contact about its own privacy practices and policies.

To be verified: operator’s full legal name, address, responsibilities, intended users, and applicable regions.
02

Information we handle

The mobile app processes information you provide, information associated with your fleet work, and information generated when you use its features.

Account & profile
Account identifiers, sign-in and contact information, company associations, driver license or CDL information, and vehicle details such as VIN and license plate.
Assigned loads
Load assignments, pickup and delivery details, trip status, and operational records associated with a driver’s work.
Location
Driver presence and route points, including coordinates, timestamps, and available heading, speed, or accuracy information. See the separate foreground and background uses below.
Messages & calls
Chat messages, shared images and files, voice messages, and signaling information used to establish real-time audio or video calls.
Documents & media
Photos, bills of lading (BOL), proof of delivery (POD), receipts, and driver credentials you upload, including optional medical certificates. These files may contain personal or sensitive information.
AI feature inputs
Driver questions, relevant current-load context, recent conversation history, and uploaded load documents when AI features are used.
Device & local data
Notification tokens and app session information, together with on-device caches for loads, chat, unsent messages, and queued location updates.

Upload only information appropriate for your fleet work and authorized for sharing. Documents and messages can contain information about other people.

03

How information is used

Information processed by the app supports its fleet operations features, including:

  • Providing account access and connecting drivers to their organization.
  • Displaying assigned loads and coordinating pickup, delivery, and trip progress.
  • Supporting driver presence, route tracking, maps, and destination navigation.
  • Delivering work-related messages, shared media, audio or video calls, and notifications.
  • Extracting information from load documents and providing AI-assisted answers when those features are used.
  • Supporting offline work and synchronizing queued information when connectivity returns.
To be verified: the purposes actually used by the operator and any applicable legal bases for processing.
04

Location & permissions

T Fleets uses location for two related purposes, subject to device permissions:

Driver presence while the app is open

For a signed-in driver, the app can update driver presence while in the foreground. These updates may occur even when the driver does not have an active load.

Background tracking during an active assignment

For an active assignment, route tracking can continue while the app is in the background. Route points include timestamps, coordinates, and available heading, speed, and accuracy information. Points collected without a connection can be queued on the device and synchronized later.

Your device permissions matter. You can review or change location access in your device settings. Limiting access may affect trip tracking and other location-dependent features.

Camera, photo library, microphone, or notification access may be requested when you use the related features. Permissions can be managed through your device settings. If you open Apple Maps or Google Maps for navigation, the selected destination is passed to that service.

05

Sharing & service providers

Work-related information may be available to authorized people in your fleet, such as dispatchers or administrators, depending on their permissions and the app’s features.

The mobile app and its supporting services use the following providers:

Supabase
Authentication, backend records, real-time updates, and storage of chat media.
Cloudinary
Hosting and delivery of uploaded profile media and driver documents.
Mapbox
Maps, routes, and geocoding. Location and destination information is processed as needed for these features.
Google & Apple
Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple push services deliver app notifications. Apple Maps or Google Maps may receive a selected destination if you choose external navigation.
OpenAI
Relevant load context, driver questions and recent history, and uploaded load documents are sent for processing when AI features run.

Communications and AI

Audio and video calling uses WebRTC with signaling information handled by the service. Voice messages are stored as part of messaging features. AI processing depends on the feature you use and the information included in your questions or documents.

To be verified: provider arrangements and processing locations, international transfers, any additional disclosures, and the operator’s commercial data practices. This draft does not make promises about provider retention or AI training.
06

Storage & retention

Information may be held in backend systems, media storage, and local app storage. The device stores session information and caches for loads, chat, unsent messages, and queued location updates to support sign-in and offline features.

The final policy will explain how long account, trip, location, message, and media records are retained, and the criteria used to determine those periods.

Retention can depend on the purpose of a record, the fleet’s operational requirements, and applicable obligations. Account closure may not remove every operational record immediately.

To be verified: retention schedules, backups, deletion handling, legal requirements, and the operator’s actual account-closure process. No retention period has been confirmed for this draft.
07

Your choices & rights

You can manage the app’s permissions in your device settings. Your fleet contact may be able to help with corrections to work-related information.

Depending on your location and the applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing.

Account and data deletion

The final policy will provide a verified way to request account and personal-data deletion and explain any information that must be retained. Requests may require identity verification.

To be verified: an accessible request channel, in-app deletion availability, who handles fleet-controlled records, applicable rights, and response procedures.
08

Security

The final policy should describe safeguards that the operator has verified and maintains for personal information. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Keep your sign-in credentials private and contact the operator if you believe your account has been accessed without authorization.

To be verified: implemented technical and organizational safeguards. This draft makes no claim of end-to-end encryption or certification.
