# Company financial analytics

`#analytics` now renders the Financial Center directly. The former ledger page, demo switch, browser-local sample dataset and demo editors have been removed. The app header and sidebar stay in place.

`AnalyticsOverview` fetches through the authenticated Supabase client and the company-scoped workspace cache. `financialCenterReader` calls `get_financial_center_snapshot` once. This STABLE, SECURITY INVOKER function reads load rows, current assignment pay, saved settings and approved settlements from one MVCC snapshot. It returns scalar JSON, so a REST row-page cap cannot silently truncate the report. There are no OFFSET pages to drift while records change. The client validates schema, counts, unique IDs, metadata and approved calculation reconciliation before replacing the previous snapshot. Existing RLS and active company-admin/dispatcher checks apply, with anonymous execution revoked. Approved settlements retain their existing admin-only access; dispatchers receive an explicit limited-access notice, not elevated permissions. Permission errors clear financial data. Network errors retain explicitly labelled prior values, with export/editing disabled until refresh succeeds.

`financialModel` excludes cancelled and trashed loads. For unconfirmed loads, Gross uses the accepted price snapshot/current contract basis; commissions and deductions use saved per-driver/company settings. For approved loads, the immutable approved document supplies Gross, company commission, per-load earnings and final statement Net, taking precedence over subsequent settings or manual pay edits. Common dispatcher/other deductions (including a signed final-Net adjustment) are allocated across the entire approved statement by Gross using largest remainder integer-cent allocation. Zero-Gross statements split deductions equally; document row order resolves equal remainders. The allocation is labelled in the load dialog, stays fixed when filters change and sums exactly to the approved final Net. PDF per-load earnings and final payout after shared deductions are distinct quantities.

Unconfirmed periodic deductions are calculated across the selected group/date period before search and driver filters. Their pay priority remains recorded accounting → matching current assignment pay → saved-rule estimate → unknown. Reports show source counts. Approved amounts mean confirmed calculation, not money paid. Dates and daily groups use the account's selected IANA timezone, reacting immediately to changes and observing DST. Preparation is memoized; maps replace repeated per-driver array scans, and searches reuse prepared calculations.

Fuel, tolls and other costs come from the load's actual accounting fields. Missing amounts remain unknown (`—`), including aggregate costs/profit; explicit zeros remain zero. The company result is labelled commission minus company costs, not total business profit. A remaining difference between settlement Gross and classified payments/fees is shown as **Корректировки / нераспределено**. Incomplete or negative allocation data is not drawn as a complete Sankey distribution. The Company view also shows additional income, trip income, broker receipts/outstanding, operating costs and trip balance. Trip income uses the original contracted load rate plus additional income; PDF-only rate edits do not change the broker's contractual balance. Trip balance is trip income minus driver final Net and company operating costs; it is not labelled net business profit. Unknown inputs propagate to the affected totals.

All five views share date, status, driver and search filters. Cards, allocation branches and chart days drill into related data. Each load dialog can export its current values to CSV, preserving unknown amounts as empty fields and neutralizing spreadsheet formulas. The page-level Export button, CSV notification and Gross explanation were removed from the main layout. Finance data remains in memory, scoped by the authenticated workspace; no browser-local demo records are read.

The driver's **Analytics** tab renders a dedicated `DriverFinancialReport` with a fixed `scopeDriver`: a single page containing four compact totals, a revenue trend, a calculation breakdown and a full load table. It does not reproduce the company page's inner navigation or Sankey. Charts, totals, status counts, load dialogs and exports are limited to that driver's ID; clearing filters cannot remove the fixed scope. Switching drivers remounts the view, clearing the previous driver's filters/dialog. It loads lazily when the Analytics tab opens and shares the authenticated workspace query with the company page. This is a report scope, not a replacement for server authorization. Periodic deductions are calculated on the same company reporting period before applying the fixed scope, so a driver's amounts reconcile with the company report.

Load accounting edits call the existing `save_load_accounting` RPC with the revision captured when editing began. Background refresh never silently substitutes a newer revision for the old draft; changed records block saving and offer an explicit reload. Escape, close, backdrop and Cancel ask before discarding dirty values, and browser reload/exit uses beforeunload. Approved pay is displayed read-only; unrelated cost edits do not rewrite the approved PDF, load contract or assignment payment. Original load details open through the existing app action. Both confirmed settlements and saved settings invalidate the analytics cache. A failed refresh after a successful save is reported separately from a failed save.
